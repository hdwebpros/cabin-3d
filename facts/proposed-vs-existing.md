# Proposed basement plan vs the real basement

Proposed plan: `source/proposed/proposed-basement-floor-plan.png` (likely made with an AI image tool; its own numbers do not add up, see `work/findings/proposed.md`).
Real basement: sheet A-1 (foundation plan and lower level), A-6 (section), and the basement photos (`work/findings/photos-basement.md`). Directions are real directions: the lake wall is south. Positions as in `cabin-facts.md` (x east from the west outside face, y north from the lake-wall outside face).

Ryan's decisions (Ryan, 2026-11-05, stated):
- The rooms on the proposed plan are fixed. The sizes can change some.
- The cold plunge is replaced by a recovery room (later: massage chairs and similar).
- The utility room and the bathroom stub-in stay where they are.
- Sauna: electric heater (needs a 240 volt circuit).
- Egress: Ryan confirmed it meets code (2026-11-05).
- Entertainment area contents and other design details come later (Phase 2).
- The lake (south) wall is a wood stud wall. It can be changed freely. All outside walls (foam walls and the lake wall) will get tongue-and-groove.
- Use the drawings for the drain locations; do not worry about the pipes under the slab.
- Stair walls (Ryan, 2026-11-05): build floor-to-ceiling walls along the open sides of the basement stair instead of a railing. The space under the stair becomes storage or a hidden room. One wall gets a Northwoods mural: misty pines, fog at the top, darker pines at the bottom.

## Summary of the big differences

1. Ceiling height: the proposed plan says 10'-0". Ryan says the basement walls are 12 ft (working value; the plans say 8'-6" clear, see conflicts.md). So 10'-0" fits. The drop ceiling hangs below the pipes.
2. The middle band does not fit across the basement. Utility 15'-6" + hall 4'-0" + Bedroom 1 14'-6" = 34'-0" side by side. The inside width is about 26'-8". Sizes to set in Phase 2. (The north band does fit; see the spa suite row.)
3. The real stair comes down into the area the proposed plan shows as the open entertainment room.
4. The center post sits where the proposed hall / Bedroom 1 wall would go. It cannot move.

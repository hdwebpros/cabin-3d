# Basement finish decisions (final)

Source: Ryan, 2026-11-05. These are settled. Use them as the default finish set in the 3D model.

| Area | Finish |
|---|---|
| Bedroom floors | Carpet |
| All other basement floors | Epoxy coating on the concrete slab |
| Walls | Tongue-and-groove boards |
| Ceiling | Drop ceiling (suspended grid) with aluminum panels |
| Stairs to the basement | Carpet, for traction (Ryan said "probably") |

## Ceiling height

- Basement walls are 12 ft tall. Ryan is 100% certain (2026-11-05). Use 12 ft.
- The plans differ: sheets A-4 and A-6 give 10'-0" slab to main floor and 8'-6" clear. Recorded in facts/conflicts.md only.
- Note for the design: the drop ceiling must be hung below the boiler pipes and the in-floor heat lines in the floor above, so the finished ceiling will be lower than the clear height.

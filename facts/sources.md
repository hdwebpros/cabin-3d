## Ryan's answers (Ryan, 2026-11-05, stated)

From STATUS.md, section "Answers from Ryan".

- Lake wall faces south; the driveway is north.
- They dug into the hill; the basement is almost fully below ground on the driveway side; the walkout to the lake is flat.
- The deck stair from the driveway at the road (north) end was built later; it is not on the plans.
- The cabin was built as drawn. The basement today has the stairs, the utility room and the bathroom stub-in; nothing else.
- The utility room and the bathroom stub-in stay. Rooms on the proposed plan are fixed; sizes can change some. The cold plunge is replaced by a recovery room. Sauna heater is electric.
- Basement walls are 12 ft tall, 100% certain; used for the 3D model (the plans differ; see conflicts.md, top item).
- Footprint 28 x 46 ft confirmed.
- Basement stair: down, landing, turn left, down more. Loft stair: up, landing, turn right, up. Both match A-1 and A-2.
- Proposed plan: the spa suite 10'-0" is the total of the sauna and cold plunge widths.
- Egress: Ryan confirmed it meets code (2026-11-05).
- Finish decisions: `facts/finish-decisions.md`.
- The 3D model runs only on Ryan's computer.

Second set, same day (given by Ryan in the session, 2026-11-05):
- The basement lake (south) wall is a wood stud wall, not concrete. It can be changed freely. It will get tongue-and-groove.
- One window, about 5 x 5 ft (memory), on each side of the basement slider: window, slider, window. Checked against the Menards order (60 x 60, agrees).
- The utility room is on the west wall. Its door is in its east wall, about in the middle of the north-south length.
- The Navien is a combi unit (boiler and instant hot water). No separate water heater. The big black tank is a whole-house water filtration system.
- No sump pit, no sewage pump. The sewer pipe leaves under the slab to the septic system. Not needed for the design.
- Top of the basement stair: open, protected by a log railing on the main level. No door.
- Pink foam board on every basement wall except the south walkout wall (the others are concrete). All will be covered with tongue-and-groove.
- Floor trusses: first about 1 ft (assumed); on 2026-11-06 Ryan chose the plan value 1'-4". Main floor check: 162" (13'-6") above the slab.
- Pipes under the slab: do not worry about them; use the drawings for drain locations.
- Ryan is trying to find photos of the basement stairs.
